Returns & Refund Policy

We want you to game with 100% peace of mind. Review our transparent refund policies for digital in-game vouchers and physical esports equipment below.

Digital Top-Up Refund Policy

Because digital game currencies (PUBG UC, Free Fire Diamonds, COD CP, eFootball Coins) are credited in real-time to game servers, completed recharges cannot be revoked once deposited.

Failed / Stuck Recharges: If an order fails to deliver due to publisher gateway errors, you are eligible for an instant 100% refund to your original payment method within 1 hour.
Unredeemed Digital Voucher Codes: If a voucher code is unredeemed, you may request a refund within 24 hours of purchase.
Incorrect In-Game UID: Refunds cannot be issued if a valid UID was provided and credited to another active player due to user typo.

30-Day Physical Hardware Returns

All physical esports gaming gear (mechanical keyboards, mice, headsets, webcams, controllers) includes a 30-day hassle-free return window.

Defective or Damaged Upon Arrival: Immediate free pickup and brand-new replacement unit dispatched within 24 hours.
Unopened & Factory-Sealed: 100% full refund if returned in original box with all accessories intact within 30 days.
Zero Restocking Fee: Unlike other stores, Tusher Gaming never charges restocking or handling penalties.
