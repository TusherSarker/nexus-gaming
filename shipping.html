Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

Tusher Gaming provides high-speed automated digital cloud delivery for in-game currencies and express nationwide courier dispatch for premium physical esports hardware.

Category 1

Digital Top-Ups & In-Game Passes

All digital game currencies (PUBG Mobile UC, Free Fire Diamonds, COD Mobile CP, eFootball Coins, Valorant Points, Roblox Robux) are processed using direct publisher API pipelines.

0 - 60 Seconds Delivery Speed
Automated cloud server deposits funds directly to your In-Game UID the moment payment clears.
100% Anti-Ban Official Receipts
Zero gray-market cards or stolen credit. Every recharge includes an official transaction reference code.
SMS & Email Dispatch Alert
You will receive an instant invoice summary and character credit confirmation.
Category 2

Physical Esports Gear & Peripherals

All keyboards, wireless mice, audiophile headsets, ergonomic chairs, and QD-OLED monitors are dispatched from our temperature-controlled central tech warehouse.

Inside Dhaka: Same-Day / 24 Hours
Orders placed before 2:00 PM are delivered the same evening via express city courier.
Outside Dhaka (All 64 Districts): 24 - 48 Hours
Shipped via Steadfast / Pathao / eCourier with real-time tracking SMS and phone verification.
International Express: 3 - 5 Days
Shipped via DHL Express or FedEx with customs documentation and insurance included.
